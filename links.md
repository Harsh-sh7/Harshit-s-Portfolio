# Update Your Links Here

To make it incredibly easy to manage your social links, I have moved all of them to a central configuration file.

1. Open `src/lib/config.js` in your code editor.
2. You will see a `socialLinks` object with placeholder URLs for all your profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn, CodeChef, Codeforces, LeetCode, etc.).
3. Replace the placeholder URLs with your actual profile links.
4. (Optional) If you have a PDF resume, drop it into the `public/` folder and name it `resume.pdf`.

Once you save the file, the links will automatically update everywhere across your portfolio (Hero section, Contact page, Footer, etc.)!
